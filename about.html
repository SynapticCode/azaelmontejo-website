About

I move between worlds: academia, tech, fitness, creative ventures, and veteran advocacy. I'm interested in how people coordinate, adapt, and build things together.

What Drives Me

I'm drawn to the intersection of systems and people. Whether it's a research lab, a veteran org, or a side project — I'm always asking the same question: how do we build things that actually work for humans?

Outside of school and research, I run a creative brand at the intersection of anime culture and nightlife. I read constantly, train consistently, and say yes to things that scare me a little.

I'm not chasing a single path. I'm building a portfolio of experiences, skills, and relationships. The throughline is curiosity.
